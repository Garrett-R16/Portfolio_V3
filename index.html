01 — Projects

Selected work

MAVERIK

Flight Computer · Gen 2

Altium · PCB Design · RF · embedded firmware

Elevate's next-generation flight computer: STM32, UWB + RF comms, three pyro channels, a 5V/8A buck, and <1% larger than previous versions. All drivers are custom because I used fancy new sensors and apparently no-one was C-pilled like I was.

Elevate

A Rocket That Can Steer Itself

control systems · sensor fusion · kalman filtering

Thrust-vector-controlled model rocket stabilized by a 2-axis gimbal and a direct-quaternion non-linear P²+I controller, which held attitude within 5° of setpoint on the final of 4 launches across 2 design iterations.

EVA

Flight Computer · Gen 1

PCB design · C/C++ · state machines

Elevate V1 Flight computer. Forced myself to route a 256 pin 0.5mm BGA on a 4 layer board (cruel and unusual, would not recommend). Specs: 2x pyros, 2x servos, IMU, NXP i.MX RT1062

High-Voltage Capacitor Charger

Power Electronics (In Design)

power electronics · magnetics · prototyping

Zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) flyback driver for rapidly charging a 15,000 µF capacitor bank to 160 VDC. Chunky custom magnetics, heavy copper plating, and a load switch for rapidly discharging said bank (don't worry about the application...)

18650 Battery Assembler

Robotics · ML

robotics · imitation learning · Python

Trained a robotic arm to assemble 18650 cells from teleoperated demonstrations using an ACT imitation-learning policy. Honorable Best AMD Hack at StarkHacks.
